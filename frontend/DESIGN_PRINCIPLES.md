# FloodLine design principles

The Figma style-guide frame is reference material, not a product screen. These principles are kept here and expressed as CSS tokens in `src/index.css`.

- Water Blue `#159BD7` carries direction, trust, and primary actions.
- Deep Water `#0876A8` supports headers and hover states.
- Orange draws attention without competing with flood severity: Soft Orange `#F59E42`, Burnt Orange `#D97724`, and Sand Orange `#FFF1E3`.
- Mist Blue `#E7F6FC` is used for soft surfaces.
- Use a compact hierarchy: 12px labels, 14–16px body copy, 18–24px headings, and 30–36px display text.
- Prefer white surfaces, 12–16px corner radii, light borders, and restrained shadows.
- Keep actions obvious, copy concise, and every flow oriented around one clear next action.
